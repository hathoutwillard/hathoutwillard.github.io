Exchange Semester at ETH Zurich

This fall, I have the wonderul oppotunity to take an exchange semester with ETH Zurich in Switzerland.

This post is currently a living document – I’ll add more to it, and update it over the course of my exchange. I’m hopeful that this can provide a useful resource for students considering an exchange at ETH.

Daily Life

Language Learning

Beyond the two week intensive German language course I’m taking at the start of the semester, all of my classes are offered in English. I think I could survive a German language course, but for important technical content, I’d rather avoid missing something critical early on.

Despite this, my german language skills should come in usefull in my Alltag, my daily life. Grocery shopping, ordering at restaurants, and hanging out with locals will all present opportunties to improve my real world understanding of the German language.

Courses

My engineering physics degree includes 15 credits (~5 classes) dedicated to “technical electives”. These are for engineering / math / physics courses that interest us, but aren’t part of the core program. These credits are what I am completing while abroad. I have dual goals of both learning material which will directly be applicable for jobs, as well as taking stuff that’s more unique and not offered at UBC.
